Every operating business — from local enterprises and e-commerce companies to multinational corporations — requires meticulous financial bookkeeping and tax compliance. Professionals skilled in computerized accounting are always essential.

## Core modules covered in practical training

- **Tally Prime Essentials:** Company creation, chart of accounts, double-entry ledger postings, and bank reconciliation statements (BRS).
- **Goods & Services Tax (GST):** CGST, SGST, IGST calculations, e-way billing, and generating GSTR-1, GSTR-3B monthly reports.
- **TDS & TCS Compliance:** Tax deducted at source provisions, rate charts, and quarterly returns.
- **Advanced Excel for Accountants:** VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, Pivot tables, financial modeling formulas, and MIS reporting.

## Career pathways

- Accounts Executive / Junior Accountant
- GST Billing & Compliance Specialist
- Audit Assistant
- Finance Operations Executive
