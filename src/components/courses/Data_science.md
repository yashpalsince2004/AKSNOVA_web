# Course Curriculum and Learning Outcomes

## Course Overview

This is a project-based professional training program focused on Data Science, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Python programming, Machine Learning, and AI-assisted workflows. The program combines theoretical foundations with hands-on assignments and real-world business case studies to help learners understand how raw data can be transformed into meaningful insights and predictive solutions.

**Program highlights:**
- **Duration:** 6 months
- **Live learning:** 280+ hours of live sessions
- **Practical exposure:** 30+ projects and case studies advertised
- **Learning approach:** Approximately 80% practical learning, as advertised
- **Core focus:** Data preparation, analysis, visualization, programming, predictive modeling, and AI-assisted productivity
- **Career preparation:** Portfolio development, resume preparation, mock interviews, doubt-resolution support, and an advertised internship component

The learning journey progresses from spreadsheet-based analytics and relational databases to Python, exploratory data analysis, visualization tools, machine learning, and AI-assisted business intelligence.

The seven core domains form a connected pathway:

**Stage 1 — Data organization:** Excel and spreadsheet analytics.

**Stage 3 — Business reporting:** Power BI and interactive dashboards.
